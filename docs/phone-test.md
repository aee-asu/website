# Phone test — launch readiness

Status: not run with real participants or physical devices. Browser emulation is separate evidence.

Ask 2–3 ASU students who did not work on the site to open the homepage on their own phones. Start each person at the homepage. Do not explain the chapter, point at controls or coach them. Use the proposed release preview before launch; repeat essential link checks on production afterward.

Ask, one at a time:

1. What does AEE do?
2. What is the next event? Where and when would you go?
3. How would you join?
4. If you worked at a company, how would you contact the chapter?

Record time to each answer, the path taken, their answer in their own words, and where they hesitated. A wrong answer matters even if they found a button quickly. Afterward ask what they expected to find but could not.

## Actual iPhone and Android production check

- Use Safari on an iPhone and Chrome on an Android phone; record model, OS, browser and date.
- Open homepage, Events, Solar Fab Field Note, Join and partnership page.
- Check menu open/close, text sizing, photo framing, landscape orientation and horizontal overflow.
- Tap join and partnership email links. Confirm the destination and prefilled subject/body in the phone's mail composer; do not send a message just to test.
- Open confirmed RSVP links and verify event identity and date. Do not submit a registration as a test.
- Share/paste the production URL into a private draft to inspect its social card, without sending it to anyone.
- Record any blocker and repeat that step after its fix.

The user authorized release with logistics, deeper takeaways and independent student/device testing deferred. Keep these items on the follow-up list. Do not describe any manual checks as complete without actual results.
